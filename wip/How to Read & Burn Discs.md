# How to Read & Burn Discs

## Introduction

Before you start your adventure with disc burning, you should know that <span style="color:#BD0000">**without** a soldered-in modchip or an exploit, no PS2 will allow any program (and therefore backup copies of games) to run from discs other than original pressed discs.</span> The only recordable media that MechaCon/Dragon (the drive controller) will accept are **DVD-Video** and **Audio CD**.

### Disc Formats

You should also keep in mind that not all disc formats are supported by every PS2 optical drive. Avoid dual-layer media, especially **±RW** discs, which for some reason put the greatest strain on PS2 lasers. Many drives also cannot read DVD+R media at all. Therefore, **DVD-R** is the safest choice.

| | |
|-|-|
| <span style="color:#009400">Supported by all models</span> | <span style="color:#009400">**PS2CD-ROM**, **PS2DVD-ROM SL/DL**, **CD-ROM**, **DVD-ROM SL/DL**, **CD-R**, <span style="color:#006600">**CD-RW**</span>, **DVD-R SL**, and <span style="color:#006600">**DVD-RW**</span></span> |
| <span style="color:#B35900">Supported by some drives</span> | <span style="color:#B35900">**DVD+M** (DVD M-Disc), **DVD+R SL/DL**, **DVD-R DL**, <span style="color:#663300">**DVD+RW**</span></span> |
| <span style="color:#BD0000">Unsupported</span> | <span style="color:#BD0000">**DVD-RAM**</span> |

### Disc Reading Issues

Your PS2 may stop reading **DVD DL** discs while still reading **CDs** and **DVD SL** discs without issues. It is also possible for the console to stop reading all DVDs before it stops reading CDs. This is often caused by a dirty lens, dirty mirrors, or dry guide rails that need lubrication. Eventually, the laser may simply become worn and require recalibration, which should always be performed using [PMAP](https://www.psx-place.com/threads/playstation-2-mechacon-adjustment-program-pmap.9530/).

### Additional notes

- **DVD5** refers to **DVD SL** (single-layer). **DVD9** refers to **DVD DL** (dual-layer). **DVD10** refers to **DVD DS SL** (double-sided, single-layer). **DVD18** refers to **DVD DS DL** (double-sided, dual-layer).
- **CDDA** (Audio CD) can be recorded onto **CD-R** or pressed as **CD-ROM**. **DVD-Video** can be recorded onto **DVD±R** or pressed as **DVD-ROM**. These are marketing terms describing the disc format, not the names of the physical media.
- DVDs can be either **8 cm** or the standard **12 cm** size (which is why the disc tray has two circular recesses).
- All **DVD+R** discs can identify themselves as **DVD-ROM** discs if the user changes the **book type** (also known as **bitsetting**) before burning the disc. It is unclear whether this improves compatibility with drives that do not officially support DVD+R media (probably not).
- Everything described above applies to **SCPH** and **KDL** models. On **DESR** models, it applies only to the **PS2 laser** assembly, not the **DVR laser** assembly.
- **ODD** stands for **Optical Disc Drive**, and **ODDW** stands for **Optical Disc Drive Writer**.
